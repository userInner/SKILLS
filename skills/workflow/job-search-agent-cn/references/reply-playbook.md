# Recruiter reply playbook

## Greeting structure

1. Name the specific capability from the job description that matches.
2. State one formal-experience proof.
3. State one shipped-product or open-source proof.
4. Invite the recruiter to review a demo, code, or resume.

Rewrite every greeting from the real job description. Do not reuse a complete template across jobs.

## Routine cases

- **Resume requested**: confirm interest, attach the latest authorized resume, and mention the two most relevant proofs.
- **Project requested**: explain the problem, personal ownership, architecture, measurable engineering outputs, and failure/recovery learning.
- **Domain gap**: state the missing experience directly, then describe transferable evidence without pretending equivalence.
- **Resume viewed**: do not reply to a platform-only status update.
- **Rejection**: mark viewed; do not argue or send an automated closing message.

## Escalate

Draft but do not send when the recruiter asks about salary, exact interview times, relocation, working hours, probation, sensitive information, offers, contracts, non-competes, equity, or an unverified claim.

Report the recruiter, company, role, exact question, and two or three concise response options to the user.

## Follow-up

Use at most one follow-up after a normal 24-hour wait. Do not follow up after an explicit rejection, hard mismatch, or request to stop.
